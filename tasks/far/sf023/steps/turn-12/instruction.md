And here, ARN has priority over every Name, Id and Scope argument, including backend selection. A same-account regional ARN must resolve in its ARN region even when called through another region's endpoint or given Scope=CLOUDFRONT; a global web ACL ARN resolves in the global backend even from a non-global endpoint with Scope omitted. Resolve the complete ARN identity, not just a matching name/id.
Malformed, non-wafv2, foreign-account, foreign-partition and nonexistent ARNs must raise WAFNonexistentItemException; none may fall back to an otherwise valid supplied Name/Id/Scope. An ARN's scope segment must be regional or global and its resource type webacl. These are explicit emulator lookup semantics, not a cross-account access feature.
When ARN is absent, keep Name/Id/Scope lookup, with scope matching the resource: a CLOUDFRONT ACL must not be found as REGIONAL just because both live in the us-east-1 backend. Both lookup forms must observe current updates and return the current LockToken. After delete/recreate under the same name, the old ARN remains nonexistent and the new ARN resolves; no stale lookup cache or fallback to a replacement is allowed. Preserve original APIs and tests; no particular internal representation is required.
