Also, Personally I have no opinion - my criteria are a) clarity and b) brevity (both subjective, I know). A "good" exception message makes it clear what the problem is as concisely as possible! Warnings, IMV, are another matter - especially `FutureWarning` s and `DeprecationWarning` s. The best recipe for these IMO is to provide the new proposed pattern (e.g. `"foo(x) is deprecated, use bar(x) instead"`).
