For the checkout at /work/w03, Switch to use ruff This PR has the project switch to using ruff instead of flake8 and handles the slight differences it has. They have a quite compelling advertisement of sorts of why you should use their system in [their readme]( but in the end I use it myself in a lot of my projects because it's got nearly all the warnings flake8 does but also has autofix support, and it's way faster.
