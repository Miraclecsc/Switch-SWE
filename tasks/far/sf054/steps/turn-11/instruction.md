Please check this too: This is supposed to fix a leak of alive but unused processes *in a very specific condition.* It happens when a process is selected from `idle_workers` *and* there is some pruning to do (i.e., there are processes that have been idle for too long). In such a case, by the time execution reaches these lines: `process` is pointing to one of the processes that were killed and removed from `idle_workers` and from `workers`, and not to the process that actually ran the request. As a result, the `if` condition fails and the process is never put back on the `idle_workers` pool, so it goes effectively untracked (i.e., never re-used again) from then on.
