One more thing: What I was thinking was instead of a `for_lock` argument, we have a `context` argument. That would take an enum that specified under what context the marker was being evaluated. We could have `metadata` which matches what it does now where `extra` is always set (and would be the default). We could have `lock_file` which doesn't have `extra` but has `extras` and `dependency_groups` always set. And then we could have `requirement` which has none of `extra`, `extras`, or `dependency_groups` automatically set. FYI I'm not attached to any of these names.
