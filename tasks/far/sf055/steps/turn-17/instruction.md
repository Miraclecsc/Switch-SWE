Also, Clarification of the bounded-mode accounting contract: count only successful uncontended async acquire() calls. acquire_nowait(), a contended completion, a recursive-acquire error, and any failed or cancelled acquisition must neither advance nor reset the count. Thus a due acquisition cancelled by native asyncio cancellation at its scheduling checkpoint must release its resource and leave the quota due for the retry. Cancellation already pending on entry must be observed even on a non-due fast acquisition. Preserve all existing handoff, ownership, waiter cleanup, capacity and statistics guarantees. These rules apply equally to Lock and Semaphore.
