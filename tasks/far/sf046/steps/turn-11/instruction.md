The implementation uses the community-sum form EQ = sum_i [ L_tilde_C / m * (k_tilde_C / 2m)^2 ] where L_tilde_C is the overlap-discounted intra-community edge count (each edge weighted by 1/(O_v * O_w) with O_v the number of communities containing v) and k_tilde_C is the overlap-discounted degree sum. This mirrors the community-sum form used by `nx.community.modularity` with overlap-discounted quantities replacing the raw counterparts. When the cover is just a partition (O_v = 1 for all v), EQ reduces to Q exactly.
