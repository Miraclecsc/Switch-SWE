And here, The "goes negative quickly" thing tripped me up too the first time. The intuition that finally stuck for me: For any community `C`, the edge-term contribution `L_C/m` is bounded above by 1 (when `C` is the whole graph), but the null-term `γ * (k_C/2m)^2` is also bounded above by `γ` (same case). So the trivial single-community cover gives `Q = 1 γ`, that is, already negative for any `γ > 1`, regardless of how clustered the graph actually is. And for non-trivial covers the edge term is smaller while the null term is still quadratic in the per-community degree sum, so the crossover into negative territory happens even faster.
