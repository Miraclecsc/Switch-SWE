Before: 10000000000000002 (wrong precision lost) After: 10000000000000003 (correct) ## Solution Modified `BaseMaskedArray.map()` to: 1. Use `to_numpy(dtype=object, na_value=pd.NA)` instead of `to_numpy()` to preserve integer values 2.
