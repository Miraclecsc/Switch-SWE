One more thing: A module-level limiter represents one persistent pool. Its explicit borrowers and total_tokens must survive sequential anyio.run calls, including switching between asyncio and Trio, and remain visible between runs. Existing off-loop reservations must count inside the next run. Async acquire_on_behalf_of and async-with use the same pool; release from a later run or outside a loop must work. Lowering total_tokens below the outstanding count creates capacity debt, without revoking borrowers. No new acquisition succeeds until enough capacity is free. Simultaneous runs, cross-thread use, and task-owned tokens surviving their owning task are outside scope.
