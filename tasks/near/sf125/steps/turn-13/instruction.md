In this part of that type, ideals are implemented on top of the module machinery, and the same failure also occurs through the submodule API, not just `R.ideal(...)`. For example, if `F = QQ.old_poly_ring(x).free_module(1)`, then `F.submodule([0], [x]).contains([x])` raises the same `ValueError`. So `sdm_groebner` needs to handle zero generators at the module level as well.
