Please check this too: Good point — putting it in `parse_tag` would make the invariant self-contained at the parsing level. The main tradeoff is that `parse_tag` is exported and used independently (e.g. `parse_tag("py3-none-any")`), though the check would still be correct there. The one practical downside of moving it: `parse_tag` can't include the full wheel filename in the error message — we'd either have `parse_wheel_filename` catch-and-re-raise with context, or accept a more generic `InvalidTag` error.
