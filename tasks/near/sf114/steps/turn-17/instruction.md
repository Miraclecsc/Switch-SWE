Also, > This seems like the right idea, but some details still need some work. > > Also - is there a reason to choose one weighted shortest path algorithm (`dijkstra`) over another (e.g. `bellman_ford`)? I preferred Dijkstra's algorithm over Bellman-Ford for weighted shortest paths due to its better performance, especially in sparse graphs with non-negative weights. However, Bellman-Ford is necessary for graphs with negative weights or to detect negative cycles. Here in NetworkX we used Dijkstra's in functions like `eccentricity` and `barycenter`, assuming non-negative weights for general-purpose graph analysis.
