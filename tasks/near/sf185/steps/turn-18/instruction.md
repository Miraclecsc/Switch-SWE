One more thing: Validate the required ABI components of recognized CPython, that type and GraalPy extension suffixes before emitting tags. A CPython suffix needs its version token; that type needs both interpreter and ABI tokens; GraalPy needs interpreter, version and execution-mode tokens. Missing or empty required tokens raise the same SystemError. Valid platform tails are excluded from the ABI as before, and unknown interpreter ABI strings retain their existing fallback behavior. Explicit abis passed to generic_tags must bypass EXT_SUFFIX discovery.
