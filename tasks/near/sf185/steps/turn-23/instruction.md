Please check this too: According to [PEP 3149]( `EXT_SUFFIX` and `SOABI` are equivalent, minus the extension. If they are not, it's a bug - and historically, `EXT_SUFFIX` is much more likely to be right: > For future reference going forward, sysconfig.get_config_var('EXT_SUFFIX') is the recommended way to spell this for CPython3.8+ and all current versions of PyPy. See CPython issues (for windows) and (for FreeBSD and AIX). There's also a chance `SOABI` will be [blank on Windows]( while `EXT_SUFFIX` is safe. Why not just use `EXT_SUFFIX`, then always strip the extension and the platform part? Then you don't need the conditional logic to see if there is a platform part.
