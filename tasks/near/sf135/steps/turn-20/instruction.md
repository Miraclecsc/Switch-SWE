And here, Pushed a small performance follow-up to `_bipartite_modularity_merge_delta`. The four degree sums were iterating over all of `G.nodes` and filtering with `v in community_a/b`, making them O(|V|) per call. Since this helper is meant to run in the inner loop of Louvain/Leiden, where candidate communities are typically much smaller than the graph. Complexity drops from O(|V|) to O(|A| + |B|) for the degree-sum step: Benchmarked on random bipartite graphs across a spectrum of community sizes, simulating the Louvain/Leiden agglomeration from singletons to late-stage merges (|V|/4).
