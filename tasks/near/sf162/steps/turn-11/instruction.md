Please check this too: This is a common practice to avoid naming conflicts. E.g. there is `ENV` variable defined by the OS, and the app itself may require `ENV` like "dev" or "prod". So you can prefix your envvars to separate app's ones and third-party. This situation pretty common in non-docker environments. Or, it may happen in docker-compose when 2 services require the same variable but with different value. These are basically two examples I recall so far.
