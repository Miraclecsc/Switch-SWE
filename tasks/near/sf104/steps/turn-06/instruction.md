One more thing: My envisaged way of running this was using an inline environment variable, like `COLUMNS=120 jupyter lab`, so that the environment variable doesn't get persisted further and affect the terminal width. Separate `JUPYTER_COLUMNS` and `JUPYTER_LINES` variables sound good to me - I just thought you might not want a proliferation of env variables in rich. If you'd prefer that approach though then it sounds great and I'm very happy to implement that way. FYI I think Jupyter Lab is increasingly taking over from Jupyter Notebook, and, unlike Notebook, Juypter Lab adapts its width to fill the screen size. Given this and the ever-increasing size of screens, I suspect that the default of 93 is probably too low nowadays.
