Apps that need this can easily add bindings in a subclass. See note in comment cmd+z is bound as an alternative to ctrl+z. Alas, not all terminals report this. Ghostty captures ctrl+z as undo for its own purposes. The cmd modifier doesn't work at all in iTerm. It does work in Kitty though. There's not much I can do from the Textual side to improve things here. It is somewhat possible to deduce what bindings are available, then at least the app can not display bindings that don't work.
