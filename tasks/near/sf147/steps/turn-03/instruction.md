Add TextArea.selection_deletion, a boolean setting defaulting to False for compatibility. When enabled, ctrl+u and ctrl+k deletion actions prioritize a nonempty selection over line-boundary logic: delete exactly the selected range in either direction, including multiline selections and selections ending at a line boundary, then collapse to its start.
